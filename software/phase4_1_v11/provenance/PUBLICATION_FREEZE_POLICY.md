# Publication freeze policy
1. No statistics are re-estimated in Phase 4.1 v1.1.
2. Figure/table curation may summarize or subset frozen upstream results only.
3. ROBUST_CORE findings may support headline associative claims.
4. CONDITIONAL_SUPPORTING findings require explicit qualification.
5. EXPLORATORY_CONTEXTUAL findings cannot be promoted into confirmatory results.
6. Formal mediation remains non-robust and cannot be described causally.
